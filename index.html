    Add Arabic to your computer

    Katib works without this, but you’ll need it to type Arabic in other apps.

    macOS

    1. Open System Settings → Keyboard.
    2. Under Text Input, click Edit…, then +.
    3. Choose Arabic, then pick Arabic or Arabic – PC.
    4. Switch layouts with Control + Space or the 🌐 key.

    Windows

    1. Open Settings → Time & language → Language & region.
    2. Click Add a language and choose an Arabic variety.
    3. Its keyboard is Arabic (101).
    4. Switch layouts with Win + Space.
